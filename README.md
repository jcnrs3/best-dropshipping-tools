## FAQ

**What's the real difference between dropshipping from US suppliers versus China?**

US and EU suppliers deliver products in 2-5 days versus 15-30 days from China, dramatically reducing refund requests from impatient customers and improving reviews. However, Chinese suppliers often charge 30-50% less than Western equivalents, enabling higher margins or more competitive pricing. The trade-off comes down to whether your customers prioritize speed over savings—premium brands justify US fulfillment, while budget stores might accept longer shipping for better margins. Testing both approaches for different product categories often works best rather than choosing one exclusively.

**Do I need to pay monthly fees to multiple dropshipping platforms or can I use free plans?**

Free plans from DSers, Spocket, Syncee, and others let you test with limited product imports (typically 10-25 items), sufficient for validating concepts before committing to paid tiers. However, serious stores quickly outgrow free limits once product count exceeds a few dozen items. Most sellers eventually subscribe to 1-3 platforms rather than attempting to manage everything free—mixing a premium platform for US products with DSers for AliExpress items provides speed and affordability without excessive subscription costs. Calculate whether subscription fees get recovered through better conversion rates and lower refunds.

**How do I avoid selling the same products as thousands of other dropshippers?**
